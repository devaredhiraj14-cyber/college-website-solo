# 🏛️ SSBT Arts, Commerce & Science College — Solo Project Website

A modern, responsive multi-page institutional portal for **Shram Sadhana Bombay Trust's Arts, Commerce & Science College, Bambhori, Jalgaon (M.S.)**.

🌐 **Live Website Link (View on any mobile/tablet/PC):**

📦 **GitHub Repository:**  
👉 **[https://github.com/devaredhiraj14-cyber/solo-project](https://github.com/devaredhiraj14-cyber/solo-project)**

## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

* **Grand Homepage (`index.html`):**
  * Sprawling 25-Acre Wi-Fi green campus presentation.
  * Direct focus on academic excellence, campus festivals, and placement partners without aggressive enquiry popups.
* **Special Presidential Tribute (`about.html`):**
  * Dedicated tribute to **Smt. Pratibha Devisingh Patil** (Former 12th President of India & First Woman President), Founder Patron of SSBT in 1983.
* **Principal's Leadership Desk:**
  * Profile and message from **Dr. G. K. Patnaik** (Principal, Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering).
* **Grand College Fests (`events.html`):**
  * **"MILESTONE"**: National-Level Technical Fest (Hackathons, Robotics arena, LAN gaming).
  * **"VASANTOTSAV"**: 3-Day Mega Cultural Gathering (Concert nights, dance battles, DJ nights).
  * **"KHEL-MAHOTSAV"**: Annual Sports Week (Floodlight cricket, football, athletics).
* **Admissions & Fee Transparency (`admissions.html`):**
  * Category-wise approved fee structure.
  * Interactive **Document Readiness Checklist** with real-time dynamic counter (*"X / 8 Documents Ready"*).
  * Collapsible FAQ accordion.
* **Statutory Compliance (`compliance.html`):**
  * Anti-Ragging Committee with 24x7 National Helpline (`1800-180-5522`).
  * Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) / POSH women's safety cell.
  * Confidential Online Grievance Redressal portal with trackable token generation.
* **Training & Placements (`placements.html`):**
  * Placement packages (₹7.50 LPA Highest), recruiter logos (TCS, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, Capgemini), and student testimonials.
* **Campus Map & Contacts (`contact.html`):**
  * Departmental phone directory and embedded interactive Google Map.
