Our Inspiring Founder & Chief Patron

Smt. Pratibha Devisingh Patil

Former President of India (12th President & First Woman President of the Republic of India)

"Youth are the real engine of national progress. By establishing institutions in semi-urban regions like Bambhori, our dream was to ensure that lack of access never hinders an ambitious mind."

Smt. Pratibha Devisingh Patil has dedicated her life to public welfare, social upliftment, women empowerment, and educational democratization. Recognizing the immense potential of rural students across Khandesh, she co-founded the Shram Sadhana Bombay Trust (SSBT) in 1983. Under her benevolent patronage, what began as a humble initiative has blossomed into one of Maharashtra's premier educational hubs with engineering, computer applications, management, and basic science streams.

About Shram Sadhana Bombay Trust (SSBT)

A philanthropic public educational trust registered under the Bombay Public Trust Act, 1950.

40+ Years of Dedication to Higher Learning

Shram Sadhana Bombay Trust was formed in the year 1983 with the primary objective of establishing educational institutions that serve students from all walks of life. The trust believes in the Gandhian principle of "Shram Sadhana" — achieving perfection through honest labor and dedicated perseverance.

The 25-acre campus at Bambhori was developed along the scenic banks of Girna river, just 8 km from Jalgaon city. It hosts lush gardens, sports grounds, residential quarters, hostels, and modern academic blocks equipped with optic-fiber Wi-Fi and advanced labs.
