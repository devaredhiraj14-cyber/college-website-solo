Chief Patron & Founder
Our Inspiring Guiding Light

Smt. Pratibha Devisingh Patil

Former President of India (12th President & First Woman to Hold Office)

"Education is the most powerful weapon which can transform rural lives, empower women, and elevate society toward supreme national progress."

In 1983, Smt. Pratibha Tai Patil along with Dr. Devisingh Shekhawat established the Shram Sadhana Bombay Trust (SSBT) with a profound vision to bring premium technical, managerial, and scientific education to the doorstep of students in Khandesh and North Maharashtra. Today, her inspiring legacy lives on through our sprawling 25-acre campus and thousands of successful alumni globally.
