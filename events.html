Annual National Tech Carnival

"MILESTONE" National Technical Symposium

MILESTONE is SSBT's flagship national-level technical extravaganza that brings together more than 3,000+ enthusiastic participants from premier universities across India. Spanning two high-octane days, students battle in cutting-edge coding competitions, build autonomous robotic machines, solve industry problems, and present innovative research papers before panels of corporate judges.

24-Hour Code-a-Thon Full stack web & mobile app building
Robo-War & Obstacle Race Custom robot arena battles
LAN Gaming Showdown Valorant, BGMI & FIFA tournaments
₹1,50,000+ Cash Pool Trophies, certificates & corporate prizes
Mega 3-Day Cultural Extravaganza

"VASANTOTSAV" Annual Gathering

Held every winter in our open-air amphitheatre, VASANTOTSAV is the ultimate celebration of student music, theatrical talent, classical art, and dance. The festival features high-voltage performances, celebrity musical concerts, traditional Maharashtrian cultural pageants (Lavani, Powada), fashion shows, and pulse-pounding DJ nights that create memories for a lifetime.

Celebrity Live Concert Renowned singers & Bollywood stars
Inter-College Dance Battles Solo, duet & mega group choreographies
Traditional & Fashion Gala Celebrating diversity & Indian heritage
High-Energy DJ Night State-of-the-art concert sound & lighting
Athletic Excellence

"KHEL-MAHOTSAV" Annual Sports Week

Spanning across our 25-acre green grounds, KHEL-MAHOTSAV tests endurance, team spirit, and sportsmanship. With full-sized cricket pitches, volleyball courts, basketball arenas, running tracks, and an indoor gymkhana, students participate passionately in inter-branch tournaments with enthusiastic cheering crowds.

Floodlight Cricket Cup T20 inter-departmental championship
Football & Volleyball Fast-paced outdoor matches
Track & Field Athletics 100m, 400m, 4x100m relay, long jump
Indoor Gymkhana Sports Badminton, Table Tennis & Chess
