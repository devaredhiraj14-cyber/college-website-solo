Flagship National Event

"MILESTONE 2026" National Tech Symposium

February 18–19, 2026 • Cash Pool: ₹1,50,000+

MILESTONE is SSBT's signature national-level technical extravaganza bringing together 3,000+ enthusiastic participants from premier universities across India. Spanning two high-octane days, students battle in 24H hackathons, robotics arena races, web sprints, and present research papers before corporate executives.

24-Hour Code-a-Thon Full stack web & mobile app building
Robo-War & Obstacle Race Custom robot arena battles
LAN Gaming Showdown Valorant, BGMI & FIFA tournaments
₹1,50,000+ Cash Pool Trophies, certificates & internship offers
Mega 3-Day Cultural Extravaganza

"VASANTOTSAV" Annual Gathering

March 5–7, 2026 • Open Air Amphitheatre

Held in our open-air amphitheatre under the stars, VASANTOTSAV is the ultimate celebration of student music, theatrical talent, traditional Maharashtrian cultural pageants (Lavani, Powada), fashion shows, and pulse-pounding EDM DJ concert nights.

Celebrity Live Concert Renowned singers & Bollywood guests
Inter-College Dance Battles Solo, duet & mega group choreographies
Traditional Heritage Gala Celebrating diversity & Maharashtra folk
High-Energy DJ Night Concert sound systems & laser lighting
Athletic Excellence

"KHEL-MAHOTSAV" Annual Sports Meet

January 12–16, 2026 • 25-Acre Sports Arena

Spanning across our 25-acre green grounds, KHEL-MAHOTSAV tests endurance, team spirit, and sportsmanship. With full-sized floodlight cricket pitches, volleyball courts, basketball arenas, running tracks, and an indoor gymkhana, students participate passionately in inter-branch tournaments with enthusiastic cheering crowds.

Floodlight Cricket Cup T20 inter-departmental championship
Football & Volleyball Fast-paced outdoor matches
Track & Field Athletics 100m, 400m, 4x100m relay, long jump
Indoor Gymkhana Sports Badminton, Table Tennis & Chess
24-Hour Codefest

"CYBER-QUEST" National Hackathon

December 22–23, 2026 • High-GPU Computer Labs

A non-stop 24-hour coding sprint where student developers from BCA, BBA, and B.Sc. streams solve real-world industry challenges across Artificial Intelligence, Web3, FinTech, and Smart Agriculture. Mentored by senior tech engineers with immediate internship offers for winning teams.
